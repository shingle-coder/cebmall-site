## What CEB Mall does

CEB Mall is a B2B supplier and sourcing partner for computer peripherals and power accessories. Instead of running an online checkout, the website works as a product catalog and inquiry channel: buyers review specifications, compare models, confirm customization details, and then request a quotation or sample.

The catalog focuses on two product families:

- **Mice** — wireless, dual-mode, silent, gaming, ergonomic and wired mice for office, home and gaming use.
- **Power products** — USB-C PD wall chargers, universal laptop adapters, 12V/5V adapter series, AC power cords and USB step-up cables.

Every product page documents the model, key specifications, supported use cases, related certifications and available media, so a buyer can shortlist models before contacting the sales team.

## Product lines

### Wireless mice

Wireless mice for office, gaming and travel, including 2.4G, Bluetooth dual-mode and rechargeable models. Representative models include S288, S289, M290, M307, M316, M330, TYY-126, TYY-127, TYY-138, TYY-213, TYY-219, TYY-30, TYY-501, SK-348 and CDK-139.

Typical options: color, logo, packaging, DPI level, button configuration and connection mode. Final configuration is confirmed per project.

### Wired mice

USB wired mice for office and entry-level retail programs, including ergonomic and compact designs. Representative models include TYY-08, TYY-12 and TYY-13.

### USB-C PD chargers and laptop adapters

Wall chargers and laptop power adapters covering 30W to 140W classes, including single-port, dual-port and built-in-cable designs. Representative models include PD65W, PD65W-LAPTOP, PD65W-DUAL, 140W, XMX-PA06515BD and XMX-1505BEPT.

### Adapter series and power accessories

Universal adapters, 12V/5V adapter series, adjustable Type-C to DC cables, USB 5V-to-9V/12V step-up cables and AC power cords. Representative models include XMX-1204, XMX-3402D, XMX-524-8, XMX-10005-6, EJ300 and EJ307.

Full model lists and specifications are published on the website product catalog.

## OEM / ODM services

CEB Mall supports buyers who need products under their own brand or with adjusted specifications. Typical customization scope:

- Logo printing and brand labeling
- Retail packaging and user manual layout
- Color, surface finish and material options
- Cable length, connector type and plug type
- Power output, port configuration and DPI settings
- Product photography and specification sheet support

Customization feasibility, tooling requirements, sample arrangement and order details are confirmed case by case. The website does not publish prices, lead times or warranty terms; these are handled by the sales team after the specification is clear.

## Quality and compliance

- Products are checked against the agreed specification before shipment.
- CE (LVD / EMC / RoHS) and FCC test reports and certificates are available for many adapter families, including the XMX-1204, XMX-3402D, XMX-524-8 and XMX-PA065xx series.
- Certificate numbers, covered models and report details are listed on the website certifications page.
- Buyers can request the documents for the exact model under evaluation before ordering.

Certification coverage differs by model. Always confirm the certificate and report for the specific model, plug type and target market before purchase.

## How buyers work with CEB Mall

1. **Browse the catalog** — shortlist models by category, application or specification.
2. **Send an inquiry** — share the model, target quantity, customization needs and destination market.
3. **Confirm the specification** — the sales team checks options, packaging and documentation.
4. **Sample and approval** — samples are arranged where required; details are agreed per project.
5. **Order and production** — order terms, inspection and shipping method are confirmed in writing.
6. **Delivery and follow-up** — shipping documents are prepared, and after-sales questions are handled by the same contact.

Inquiries receive an immediate automated acknowledgement with no quotation or commitment, followed by a human reply, normally within 24 hours.
